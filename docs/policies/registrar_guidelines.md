# University Registrar & Administrative Guidelines

## 1. Medical and Duty Leave Policy
- Students must submit medical leave requests at least 24 hours in advance or within 48 hours of returning.
- Medical leaves exceeding 3 days require an attached doctor's certificate.
- Duty leaves for hackathons, sports meets, or cultural fests require prior faculty coordinator approval and official event invitation letters.

## 2. Placement & Internship Eligibility Rules
- To sit for Tier-1 companies (like TechCorp), students must maintain a minimum CGPA of 7.5.
- Students must have zero active backlogs at the time of application.
- Attendance across the preceding semester must not fall below 75%.

## 3. Examination Seating & Hall Tickets
- Hall tickets are generated 48 hours prior to the start of mid-term and end-term examinations.
- Students must verify their block and room allocation in advance via the student portal or Campus Companion AI.
